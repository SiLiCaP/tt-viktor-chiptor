Why the exchange takes a second here and ~3 ms on the real board: every pin word crosses USB, while on the Tiny Tapeout board the RP2350 drives the pins directly with PIO and DMA at the chip's clock.

A SHA-3 coprocessor for post-quantum crypto on Tiny Tapeout. The chip is one Keccak-f[1600] sponge: the 1600-bit state lives in clock-gated shift rings and moves 8 slices per clock, so a permutation takes 368 clocks (7.4 µs at 50 MHz). It runs ML-KEM's four hash functions and, on the way out, its CBD noise sampler.
